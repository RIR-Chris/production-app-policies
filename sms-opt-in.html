Roof It Right, LLC Text Messaging Terms and Opt-In

Last updated: 2026-10-07

Roof It Right, LLC sends service-related text messages to customers who ask us to. This page explains the programme, how customers opt in, and how to stop.

How customers opt in

We ask for consent before sending any message. Customers give it by phone, in person, or in a signed service agreement, and the language we use is the same in every case — it is printed below and we read it out or show it before the first message is sent.

The consent language shown to the customer:

By providing my mobile number and agreeing to receive text messages from Roof It Right, LLC at the number provided, I consent to receive text messages from Roof It Right, LLC. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. See our Privacy Policy and Terms and Conditions.

What we send

  • Appointment reminders and scheduling updates
  • Job and service status updates
  • Account notifications, including invoices and payment reminders
  • Customer-care and service follow-up

We do not send marketing messages to anyone who has not asked for them, and we do not sell or rent personal information.

How to stop

  • Reply STOP to any message to unsubscribe. You will receive one final message confirming your opt-out, and no further messages.
  • Reply HELP for assistance, or contact us using the details below.
  • Opting out of text messages does not affect other ways we communicate with you.

We do not share mobile numbers

We do not share mobile phone numbers. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Message frequency and rates

Message frequency varies based on your active service requests. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

How consent is recorded

Every opt-in is recorded in our customer system with the date, time and the source of the consent (web form submission, phone call, in-person agreement, or signed contract), so consent can be evidenced on request.
