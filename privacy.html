SMS Consent and Opt-Out

We send text messages only to customers who have given us prior express consent. We ask for it by phone, in person, or in a signed service agreement, and the language we use is the same in every case:

The consent language shown to the customer: "By providing my mobile number and checking this box, I agree to receive text messages from Roof It Right, LLC at the number provided. Consent is not a condition of purchase. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help."

We do not share mobile phone numbers. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent are not shared with any third parties.

Consent is recorded with its date, time and source, and can be evidenced on request. Full details, including what we send and how to stop, are on our Text Messaging Terms and Opt-In page.

  • Opting out of SMS messages will not affect other communication methods unless specifically requested
